# Picture sources

## hero-schuylkill-night.jpg

"Philadelphia Skyline" by Andrew Parlette, taken 2023-03-13 from the South
Street Bridge. Licence: CC BY 2.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?curid=135482068

Changed: the left 27% of the original (12058 x 4823) is cropped off, and the
rest is scaled to 4800 x 2630. `scripts/build-hero.mjs` makes the served sizes
from that, and for phones also crops a 1215 x 2630 column (from x 1601) around
the two tallest towers. The social image (`app/opengraph-image.png`) is a
screenshot of the first screen, so it carries the same photograph. The page
credits it in the footer, with its title, author, licence and "cropped".

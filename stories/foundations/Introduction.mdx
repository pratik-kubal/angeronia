# angeronia.com

The site of Angeronia Labs LLC: one page that sells fixed-scope AI agent work on the Claude API, plus the privacy and terms pages. Every section is seen here, as a story, before it is part of a page; `Pages/Home` is the page itself.

The contract is `docs/design-system/DESIGN-RULES.md`. Every decision, and every deviation from it, is in `docs/design-system/DECISIONS.md`.

The specification the pages were built from is `docs/plans/monument-redesign/`: `design.md` for the look, `content.md` for every word.
